Fantasy Football Universe

FFU Data API

Player values, completed trades and manager trading behavior from the FFU database: every trade in the leagues we track, valued on the day it happened. Built for platforms, tools and creators who want values grounded in what managers actually pay.

Getting a key. Keys are issued by FFU during the partner beta. Email hello@fantasyfootballuniverse.com. Send it on every request in the x-api-key header. Each key has a daily limit (UTC); responses carry X-RateLimit-Limit and X-RateLimit-Remaining. 
curl -H "x-api-key: YOUR_KEY" "https://fantasyfootballuniverse.com/api/v1/values?shape=sf"

Endpoints

GET/api/v1/values?shape=sf|oneqb

Today's FFU value for every player and pick. Optional day=YYYY-MM-DD for a past day; src=trades,market (default) filters by how each value was set.

{ "meta": { "day": "2026-11-23", "shape": "sf", "count": 671 },
  "data": [ { "asset": "9221", "ffu": 10912, "market": 10751, "factor": 1.015, "n": 214, "src": "trades" }, … ] }
GET/api/v1/players/{sleeperId}?shape=sf&days=90

One player's daily FFU value history, newest first.

GET/api/v1/managers/{sleeperUserId}

How a manager trades this season, across every league in the database: trades, leagues, partners, the share of value they take in and send out by position, and their pricing (median gap, each trade valued on its own day; positive means they give more than they get).

GET/api/v1/trades?player={id} | owner={userId} | league={id}

Recent completed trades, newest first, with who got what. limit up to 200.

GET/api/v1

Status, endpoints and the latest values day. No key needed.

How values are made

FieldMeaning
ffuThe FFU value: market × factor.
marketThat day's market price for the league shape.
factorWhat real trades in the FFU database pay against the market price, fit nightly with a pull toward 1.00 where trades are few. Checked every night on trades the fit never saw.
nTrades behind the factor.
srctrades (5+ trades behind it) or market (fewer).

Shapes: sf = Superflex / 2QB, oneqb = one quarterback. Player ids are Sleeper ids; picks are 2027-1, with tiers 2027-1-early / -mid / -late. Partner beta: endpoints and fields may still change, with notice. Data may not be resold without an FFU data license.
